Reading this table. The line under each bid number shows only the items that matched your categories — not the full bid contents. A bundled bid badge means GeM packaged several products into one tender; hover it to see everything included. In that case the quantity carries an asterisk* because GeM publishes only a bid-wide total covering every line item, not just the computers. Per-product quantities exist only inside the bid PDF. “Bid Start Date” is when bidding opens, not the document publication date. Reverse auctions are excluded — only open tenders are listed. Bids are grouped by month — click a month to expand or collapse it. The view defaults to open bids of 5+ units — single-unit tenders are hidden by default. Use the quantity and status filters to widen it. Bids that closed more than 7 days ago are not stored in the page at all — the full history (3,467 bids) is in bids.csv. State is read from the buyer’s department name; bids marked central are national bodies such as Military Affairs or Indian Railways that are not tied to one state.
